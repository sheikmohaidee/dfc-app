Rules that hold everywhere
The short list worth arguing about.
Unstructured in, structured out. Every voice note or photo resolves into a bordered template card within one screen. The AI never answers a photo with a paragraph.
Show the doubt. Anything the model is unsure of carries a VERIFY chip and a question mark on its price — and becomes a human's job in the next column.
Touch targets. 44px minimum anywhere a finger goes; 52px for calls; 70px for rider status. Steppers may be 30px inside a 48px row.
One primary per screen. Black fill is scarce. If two buttons are black, one of them is wrong.
Colour is meaning. A hue only ever says which category or which state. Nothing is coloured for decoration.
No fake chrome. No painted status bars, no drawn keyboards, no invented battery icons in any mockup.
